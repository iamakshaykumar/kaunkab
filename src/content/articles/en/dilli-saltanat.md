## Background

At the end of the 12th century, **Muhammad Ghori** made several invasions of north India. In 1192, in the Second Battle of Tarain, he defeated Prithviraj Chauhan. After Ghori's death in 1206, his general **Qutb-ud-din Aibak** began independent rule in Delhi. The **Delhi Sultanate** is considered to begin from here.

## The Five Dynasties

From 1206 to 1526, five dynasties ruled Delhi in succession:

1. **The Mamluk (Slave) dynasty**: Aibak, Iltutmish, Razia Sultan, Balban
2. **The Khilji dynasty**: Alauddin Khilji
3. **The Tughlaq dynasty**: Muhammad bin Tughluq, Firoz Shah Tughlaq
4. **The Sayyid dynasty**
5. **The Lodi dynasty**: Ibrahim Lodi was the last sultan

## Major Rulers and Events

- **Iltutmish** strengthened the Sultanate and carried forward the construction of the Qutb Minar.
- **Razia Sultan** (1236–1240) was the first woman sultan to sit on the throne of Delhi.
- **Alauddin Khilji** checked the Mongol invasions and made rules to control prices in the market.
- **Muhammad bin Tughluq** carried out experiments such as moving the capital from Delhi to Daulatabad (Maharashtra) and introducing token copper currency, which failed.

## Society and Culture

In this period Persian became the language of administration. The use of arches and domes increased in architecture. Bhakti and Sufi saints spread a message of love and devotion among the people, and poets such as **Amir Khusrau** composed works in Hindavi.

## The End

In 1526, at the First Battle of Panipat, **Babur** defeated Ibrahim Lodi. With this the Delhi Sultanate came to an end and the Mughal Empire began.
