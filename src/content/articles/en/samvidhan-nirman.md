## Background: The Cabinet Mission and the Formation of the Constituent Assembly

In 1946, on the basis of the recommendations of the British Cabinet Mission, an independent Constituent Assembly was formed for India. The Assembly was indirectly elected by the provincial legislatures and represented all of India's religions, classes and regions, and included 15 distinguished women.

On **9 December 1946**, the historic first meeting of the Constituent Assembly was held in the Constitution Hall (the Central Hall of Parliament) in New Delhi. The senior-most member, Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha, was elected interim president, and on 11 December **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** was elected permanent president.

On 13 December 1946, Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru moved the historic **'Objectives Resolution'**, which later became the basis of the 'Preamble' of the Constitution.

## Dr. Ambedkar and the Drafting Committee

The most important role in framing the Constitution was played by the **Drafting Committee**, formed on 29 August 1947. **Dr. Bhimrao Ramji Ambedkar**, the chief jurist of modern India, was made its chairman.

On the preliminary draft prepared by Sir B.N. Rau (the Constitutional Adviser), Dr. Ambedkar and the committee members made a deep study of democratic constitutions around the world (the fundamental rights of the USA, the parliamentary system of Britain, the directive principles of Ireland) and adapted them to Indian conditions.

To root out any discrimination on the basis of caste, sex or religion, Dr. Ambedkar made **Article 17 (abolition of untouchability)** and the fundamental rights the heart and soul of the Constitution (Article 32).

## Historic Debates and Deliberations

Framing the Constitution took a total of **2 years, 11 months and 18 days**. During this time there were 11 sessions and 165 sittings.
- Every article and word of the Constitution was openly debated, and more than 2,400 amendments were discussed in detail.
- In the delicate period of the tragedy of Partition and the integration of more than 560 princely states, Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel made the federal structure unbreakable and strong.
- The bold decision of Universal Adult Suffrage was taken, giving every Indian citizen the equal right to vote from the very first day of independence.

## Adoption and the Day of 26 January 1950

On **26 November 1949** the Constituent Assembly formally passed and adopted the Constitution. That is why 26 November is celebrated every year as **'Constitution Day'**.

On 24 January 1950 the members signed the original manuscript. To make the memory of the historic 'Purna Swaraj Day' of 1930 lasting, the Constitution came fully into force across the country on **26 January 1950**, and India became a sovereign democratic republic. (The words "socialist" and "secular" were added to the Preamble later, by the 42nd Amendment of 1976.)
