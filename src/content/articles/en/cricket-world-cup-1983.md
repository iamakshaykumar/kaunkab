## A Team Considered Weak

The **1983** Cricket World Cup, called the **Prudential Cup** after its sponsor, was played in England and Wales from **9 to 25 June**. It was the third World Cup. Eight teams took part and each innings was of **60 overs**; players wore white clothing and played with a red ball.

Both earlier World Cups — **1975** and **1979** — had been won by **West Indies**, whose team had **Clive Lloyd**, **Vivian Richards**, **Gordon Greenidge** and a deadly quartet of fast bowlers. India had won only one match in total in the previous two World Cups. Bookmakers offered odds of **66 to 1** on an Indian win. The team's captain, **Kapil Dev**, was only 24 years old.

The team included players such as **Sunil Gavaskar**, **Krishnamachari Srikkanth**, **Mohinder Amarnath**, **Yashpal Sharma**, **Sandeep Patil**, **Kirti Azad**, **Roger Binny**, **Madan Lal**, **Syed Kirmani**, **Balwinder Sandhu**, **Ravi Shastri**, **Dilip Vengsarkar** and **Sunil Valson**. Most of the Indian bowlers were medium-pacers, skilled at swinging the ball in England's damp conditions. Several all-rounders gave the team a good balance.

## The Group Matches and Kapil's 175

India stunned everyone by beating the reigning champions **West Indies** by **34 runs** in their very first match — West Indies' first ever defeat in a World Cup. But the road ahead was not easy. After losses to Australia and West Indies, India faced the threat of elimination.

On **18 June 1983**, at **Tunbridge Wells** against **Zimbabwe**, India's score was just **17 for 5**. Then Kapil Dev came out to bat and played a memorable innings of **175 not out**. It was then the highest individual score in one-day international cricket. A big ninth-wicket partnership with **Syed Kirmani** took India to **266/8**, and India won by **31 runs**. Then, in the last group match, India beat **Australia** by **118 runs** to reach the semi-finals; **Roger Binny** and **Madan Lal** took four wickets each.

## The Semi-final — Victory over Hosts England

On **22 June**, at **Old Trafford** in Manchester, India faced hosts **England** in the semi-final. The Indian bowlers restricted England to 213, and with half-centuries from **Yashpal Sharma** and **Sandeep Patil**, India won by **6 wickets**. All-rounder **Mohinder Amarnath** was Man of the Match. India had reached a World Cup final for the first time.

## The Final at Lord's — 25 June 1983

The final was played on **25 June 1983** at the historic **Lord's** ground in London. West Indies won the toss and sent India in to bat. The Indian batsmen could not hold out against the fast bowlers and the whole team was out for **183 in 54.4 overs**. **Krishnamachari Srikkanth** made the highest score, **38 runs**. Most people believed West Indies would reach this target easily.

But the Indian bowlers worked wonders:

- **Balwinder Sandhu** bowled **Gordon Greenidge** with a ball that came in.
- **Vivian Richards** (33 runs), who was scoring fast, hit a high shot off **Madan Lal**, and **Kapil Dev** took a superb catch running backwards. This is regarded as the decisive turning point of the match.
- **Madan Lal** and **Mohinder Amarnath** took three wickets each; Amarnath took 3 wickets for just **12 runs** and also made 26 runs with the bat.

The entire West Indies team was out for **140 in 52 overs**, and India won by **43 runs**. Amarnath was **Man of the Match** for the second match in a row. The picture of Kapil Dev holding the World Cup trophy on the balcony at Lord's became one of the most memorable images in Indian sporting history.

## The Impact of the Victory

This victory turned cricket into a passion in India. Coming at a time when television was reaching homes across the country, this success inspired millions of children to play cricket. India's confidence grew, and so did its influence in world cricket. The **1987** World Cup was held outside England for the first time, jointly in India and Pakistan.

India later won the one-day World Cup again in **2011**, but the 1983 victory has a special place — it is the story of a team that nobody considered a contender. The film **'83'** was made on this story in **2021**. Kapil Dev's "Kapil's Devils" showed that with confidence and team spirit even the biggest team can be beaten.
